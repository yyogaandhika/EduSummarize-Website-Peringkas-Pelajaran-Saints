# EduSummarize visual direction

- Identity: editorial learning tool for Indonesian biology and science materials, serving SMP/SMA students.
- Personality: warm, focused, trustworthy, curious; professional without feeling institutional.
- Palette: deep botanical green for primary actions and science identity; warm paper for the canvas; charcoal ink for readable content; restrained terracotta accent for emphasis.
- Typography: Georgia for display headings to evoke a study guide; system sans-serif for controls and body copy for reliable readability.
- Composition: content-first pages, strong typographic hierarchy, editorial alignment, varied section rhythm; avoid dashboard chrome and decorative imagery.
- Motion: restrained transitions only; motion clarifies state changes.
- ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.
- Decision reasons: botanical green connects to biology while staying calm; warm paper supports long-form reading; serif display headings distinguish learning content from controls; layout prioritizes the actual summarization workflow; minimal motion keeps study text steady.
